DarkMatterFM-Registry

A machine-readable registry of beyond-the-Standard-Model dark-matter models extracted from the arXiv literature. Every model is a structured card recording what the model is, which observables and constraints apply, which computational routes evaluate it, and what is still missing to evaluate it. This site hosts the model cards and a wiki for browsing them, the schema suite and its browser, and the capability-gap roadmap with the research projects derived from it.

Interactive viewers

Model wiki

Browse and search all 6,145 model cards with a full detail pane per card: facets by family, model kind, status and portal, and links to the paper-status, ranking and enrichment viewers.

Schema browser

All seven schema files as a navigable tree: every field with its description, type, enum values, examples and required flag; cross-file references resolved; search; a permalink per field.

7 files · 528 fields

Research-project ranking

25 concrete projects, one per missing capability, ranked by impact, structure-formation impact, cost and value ratio, with the highest-priority models each would unblock.

25 projects · 2,004 models

Capability-gap roadmap

The 5,166 capability-gap entries grouped into 150 themes and weighted by the priority of the models they block, with every member model listed per group.

Schemas

  • bsm_model.schema.yamlthe model card: 31 top-level sections
  • common.schema.yamlshared definitions (parameters, ranges, assumptions, agent warnings, status)
  • observable.schema.yamlone observable channel
  • constraint.schema.yamlone constraint with its assumptions and robustness grade
  • route.schema.yamla theory-to-observable route: solvers, emulators, likelihoods, step status
  • provenance.schema.yamlsources, extraction and review status per claim
  • code.schema.yamlassociated code and execution environments
  • schemas/README.mdhow the files fit together
