How the scores are computed

Impact
sum of the registry priority scores of the distinct models the project unblocks, each weighted by how badly it is blocked (cannot be tested at all ×1.0 · key observables blocked ×0.8 · precision degraded ×0.4 · minor ×0.1).
SF impact
the same sum, counting only gaps that block a structure-formation observable (Lyman-α, satellites, dwarfs, lensing, clusters, small- and large-scale structure).
Cost
mean effort of the project’s gap entries (small = 1, medium = 3, large = 10, multi-year program = 30; roughly person-months).
Value
impact ÷ cost — science unblocked per unit of effort.
